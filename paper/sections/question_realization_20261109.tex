\subsection{Question realization with fixed SQL}\label{sec:realization}
A preliminary comparison finds 1,973 Ring--Template pairs sharing SQL, domain and schema prefixes but differing in questions. Ring's execution-match rate is $[-13.96,-13.73]$ pp lower, motivating a controlled wording intervention.

We retain all 2,000 first-release Refill SQLs and quotas with Ring-style Qwen2.5-7B realization. The plan and 160-item audit (20/domain) precede generation. It yields 1,999 format-valid questions and 19,990 predictions; the remaining item stays unknown.




Rewriting improves Verified gap in all eight domains (Fig.~\ref{fig:construction-effects}(d)) and aggregate Base gap, while reducing source fidelity (Table~\ref{tab:realization-results}). Concordances increase, but regret intervals overlap. Against Ring, rewritten questions retain lower TEF with unresolved gap difference.
