\subsubsection{Question Generation}\label{sec:realization}
To isolate the role of wording, we hold SQL fixed. A preliminary comparison finds 1,973 Ring--Template pairs sharing SQL, domain and schema prefixes but differing in questions. Ring's execution-match rate is $[-13.96,-13.73]$ pp lower, motivating a controlled wording intervention.

We retain all 2,000 first-release S2B SQLs and quotas and use Ring-style Qwen2.5-7B question generation. The plan and 160-item audit (20/domain) precede generation. It yields 1,999 format-valid questions and 19,990 predictions; the remaining item stays unknown.

Rewriting improves Verified gap in all eight domains and aggregate Base gap, but worsens Verified TEF (Table~\ref{tab:realization-results}). Concordances increase; regret intervals overlap. Against Ring, TEF is lower and gap unresolved.
