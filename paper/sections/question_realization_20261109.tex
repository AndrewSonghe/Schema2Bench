\subsection{Question realization with fixed SQL}\label{sec:realization}
We vary question wording while fixing SQL to compare its effects on the two objectives. A preliminary comparison finds 1,973 Ring--Template pairs with identical SQL and schema prefixes in the same domain but different questions. Ring's execution-match rate is $[-13.96,-13.73]$ pp lower. This motivates the wording intervention, with semantic agreement assessed separately.

We retain all 2,000 first-release Refill SQLs, databases and quotas, using the recorded Ring-style Qwen2.5-7B realization configuration. The plan and 160-item audit sample (20/domain) are fixed before generation. There are 1,999 format-valid questions and 19,990 predictions; the remaining item stays unknown without replacement.




Rewriting moves Refill toward Ring in target alignment and away from original Refill in source fidelity (Table~\ref{tab:realization-results}). Verified gap improves in all eight domains (Fig.~\ref{fig:construction-effects}(d)), and the aggregate direction also holds under Base scoring. Definite concordance counts increase. The regret intervals overlap and do not establish a direct selection benefit. The Ring comparison captures the tradeoff: rewritten questions retain lower TEF, but the target-gap difference crosses zero.
